Running plan (beginner → 2 km)

Every run starts with a 5 min brisk walk plus dynamic stretches (leg swings, high knees). It ends with a 5 min walk and 5 min of stretches. Run at a pace where you can still talk. Pain is a stop sign; normal soreness is fine.

Vegetarian diet

  • 6:45 am, on waking: 1 glass of warm water + 5 soaked almonds + 2 walnuts
  • Before the run (if you feel hungry): half a banana
  • 8:00 am, after the run: Juice/smoothie (see below) + breakfast: 2 besan/moong chillas, OR poha with peanuts, OR oats upma, OR 2 paneer parathas with curd
  • 1:00 pm lunch (tiffin): 2 rotis + dal/rajma/chole + sabzi + salad + curd
  • 4:30 pm snack: roasted chana / sprouts chaat / fruit / buttermilk. No samosas or biscuits.
  • 8:30 pm dinner: light. 1–2 rotis + paneer/soya/dal + sabzi, OR khichdi + curd. Eat at least 2.5 hours before sleeping.
  • 10:30 pm (optional): a glass of haldi doodh (turmeric milk)
  • Water: 3 litres a day. Keep a bottle on your office desk.
  • Protein every day: paneer, dal, curd, sprouts, soya chunks, peanuts. Aim for 60–70 g.
  • Cut down: sugar, cold drinks, maida, fried food, late-night snacking

🥤 Blender/juicer menu (rotate daily)

  • Mon/Thu, ABC juice: 1 apple + 1 beetroot + 2 carrots + a little ginger (stamina, iron)
  • Tue/Fri, banana-peanut smoothie: 1 banana + 1 tbsp peanut butter or 10 peanuts + 1 glass milk + 1 tbsp oats (recovery, protein)
  • Wed/Sat, green juice: spinach + cucumber + 1 apple + lemon + mint
  • Sun, amla-carrot: 2 amla + 2 carrots + ginger + a little honey (immunity in winter)
  • Afternoon at office: buttermilk with jeera

Don't strain the juice; the fibre is good for you. Drink it fresh, within 15 minutes.
