          🥤 Juice of the day (blender/juicer)

          Don't strain the blender ones (fibre is good for you). No sugar. Drink fresh. Don't drink juice on an empty stomach if you get acidity; have 2–3 almonds first.

          ✨ Pigmentation: what actually works

          Honest truth: juice alone will not remove pigmentation. What fades it most is sunscreen every day plus a simple routine. Diet and juice help from the inside. You'll see a visible improvement in 6–8 weeks, and pigmentation fades fully over 3–6 months. If the patches are brownish and symmetrical on the cheeks or forehead (possible melasma), or there's no change in 6–8 weeks, see a dermatologist once. They can prescribe proper creams.

          ☀️ Morning (after bath, 2 min)

          1. Gentle face wash (no scrubbing)
          2. Niacinamide 5% serum OR vitamin C serum (2–3 drops)
          3. Light moisturizer
          4. Sunscreen SPF 50, PA+++: two finger-lengths for face + neck. Reapply at 1:30 pm in the office as well (sunlight from windows counts too)

          1. Face wash (wash twice if you wore sunscreen)

          🍊 Foods for skin (include every day)

          • Vitamin C: 1 amla / 1 orange / 1 guava / lemon in dal. Reduces melanin and helps collagen
          • Lycopene: cooked tomato (sabzi/soup), protects against sun damage
          • Beta-carotene: carrot, papaya, pumpkin
          • Vitamin E + zinc: 5 almonds + 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds + 1 tbsp sunflower seeds
          • Omega-3: 2 walnuts + 1 tsp flax/chia
          • Green tea: 1–2 cups (instead of a sugary chai)
          • Avoid: sugar, fried food, too much milk-sweet stuff. Sleep by 12 (skin repairs at night)

          ❌ Don't do

          • Lemon / baking soda / toothpaste directly on the face
          • Scrubbing daily, picking pimples
          • Going out in the sun without sunscreen or a cap
          • Using several new products at once (patch test first)
