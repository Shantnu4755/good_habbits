📵 Phone detox: priority #1

📱 Screen time kasa baghaycha (which app)
  1. Android (built-in, free): Digital WellbeingSettings → Digital Wellbeing & parental controls → the number at the top is today's screen time. Tap "Show your data" to see per-app usage. App timers + Bedtime mode (grayscale) are here too. On Samsung: Settings → Digital Wellbeing and parental controls.
  2. iPhone (built-in, free): Screen TimeSettings → Screen Time → "See All App & Website Activity". Set limits in App Limits, and grayscale is in Accessibility → Display → Colour Filters.
  3. Extra help (free): "one sec" appBefore Instagram/YouTube opens, it makes you take a deep breath and asks "do you really want to open this?". Very effective for breaking the habit. Play Store / App Store.
  4. Android detailed stats (optional): StayFreeFree, shows screen time + unlock count + a weekly graph. Not needed if Digital Wellbeing is enough.
  5. Steps sathiAndroid: Google Fit or Samsung Health · iPhone: the Health app (built-in). Write the steps number in the tracker at 11 pm.

      ⏱ Race goal (2 km timed)

      Age 26, beginner: a realistic target is 12:00 (6:00/km), stretch goal 11:00. After the 26 Dec time trial, set your goal = that time minus 20–30 seconds.

      🏁 Race day strategy

      1. 2 hours beforeBanana + 1 toast/poha, 1 glass water. Nothing new, no heavy food.
      2. Warm-up (15 min before)5 min jog + leg swings + 3 strides (fast but relaxed 50 m).
      3. First 500 mDon't go off too fast with the crowd. Run 3–5 sec/400 m slower than your goal pace.
      4. 500 m – 1500 mHold goal pace. Breathe steadily (2 steps in, 2 steps out).
      5. Last 500 mGive it everything 🔥. Pump your arms. Kick in the last 200 m.
      6. Shoes + kapdeWear the same shoes you trained in. Pin your bib the night before.

      🏃 Running plan

      • Jog mhanje kay?A pace where you can still talk easily. If you can't speak a full sentence, slow down. Speed comes later.
      • Week cha patternMon easy · Wed speed/intervals · Fri tempo · Sat long run / time trial · Tue + Thu strength · Sunday rest. Time trials: 28 Nov (baseline), 12 Dec, 26 Dec → final race
      • Every runWarm-up (8 min) → main run → cool-down (8 min). Never skip these, they prevent injuries.
      • Pain ruleNormal soreness is fine. Sharp pain in a knee, shin or ankle = stop, and just walk that day. Running shoes are a must (not chappals or flat sneakers).
