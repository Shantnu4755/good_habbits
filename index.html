🏃 Running plan

  • Jog mhanje kay?A pace where you can still talk easily. If you can't speak a full sentence, slow down. Speed comes later.
  • Week cha patternRun on Mon/Wed/Fri/Sat · Strength A on Tue · Strength B on Thu · Sunday rest + walk
  • Every runWarm-up (8 min) → main run → cool-down (8 min). Never skip these, they prevent injuries.
  • Pain ruleNormal soreness is fine. Sharp pain in a knee, shin or ankle = stop, and just walk that day. Running shoes are a must (not chappals or flat sneakers).

                  The honest truth: juice helps from the inside, but what fades pigmentation most is sunscreen every day. You'll see a clear difference in 6–8 weeks, and pigmentation fades fully over 3–6 months. If the patches are brownish on both cheeks or the forehead, or nothing changes in 6 weeks, see a dermatologist once.
